FIRST Timeline

A historical timeline of FIRST Robotics, it's key teams during the early years and an archive of rediscovered media.

  • Key Features
  • Contact

Project Overview

Documenting a timeline of events of the first events, teams, match data, robot photos, etc. of FIRST Robotics. There will be a key aspect of celebrating the history of one of the most prolific non-profits and their goal to push STEM education, as well as the teams that have stuck with the program since it's conception.

Key Features

  • Interactive Timeline: Visual representation of the years that FIRST has been running.
  • Landmark Events/Teams: Information blurbs and additional pages for key historical events, teams that formed, and other FIRST history.
  • Data!: Key data-points from throughout FIRST's History, from the year they expanded globally, the year they hit their first 1000 competing teams, etc.
